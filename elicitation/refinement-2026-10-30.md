# Backlog refinement with the client, 2026-10-30

- **Medium:** Microsoft Teams, recorded with transcription. The recording is kept outside this repository.
- **Participants:** Ing. Fabrizzio Ontaneda Valverde (client) and Team 1.
- **Purpose:** review the user stories of backlog v2 one by one before the midterm SRS.

## Outcomes

| Story | Outcome |
|---|---|
| US-01 | Signing in with a Google or Microsoft account is left for a future version. |
| US-03 | Staff accounts are registered with name, email and role. |
| US-05 | The patient record holds name, date of birth, contact data and reason for consultation (required fields still pending in OQ-03). |
| US-07 | Every change to a patient record keeps who made it and when. |
| US-15, US-17 | The structured note is shown next to the therapist's original text, which is never lost. |
| US-18 | The previous-session summary keeps the 5-line limit (OQ-11), is built only from approved notes, and says when there is no previous session. |
| US-19 | Notes are listed newest first with date, format and status; opening one shows the original text and the structured version; notes cannot be deleted. |
| US-21, US-22 | The therapist can add, edit or remove symptom tags; corrections are reflected in the evolution chart, which uses approved notes only. |
